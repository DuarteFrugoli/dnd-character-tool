# Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 6, 2026

D&D Character Tool is developed by Pedro Henrique Duarte Frugoli.

This app is a character creation and management tool for tabletop role-playing games.

## Data Collection

D&D Character Tool does not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share personal user data.

The app works locally on the user's device. Character information created inside the app is stored only on the user's device and is not sent to the developer or to any external server.

## Internet Access

The current version of the app does not require an account, login, cloud synchronization, or online database.

## Third-Party Services

The current version of the app does not use third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, crash reporting SDKs, or payment services.

If third-party services are added in the future, this Privacy Policy will be updated to describe what data may be collected and how it is used.

## Data Retention and Deletion

Because the app stores data locally on the user's device, users can delete their data by deleting characters inside the app, clearing the app data, or uninstalling the app.

## Children's Privacy

The app is not designed to collect personal information from children.
